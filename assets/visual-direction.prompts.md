# Built-in ImageGen production prompts

## Sunset lake

Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: full-width environment background for a pixel-art fishing game.
Primary request: Create a beautifully detailed pixel art sunset mountain lake matching the LEFT PANEL of the reference. Environment only, no interface.
Style: crisp visible square pixels, stepped edges, rich limited palette, layered nostalgic RPG pixel art. Golden orange and pink sunset, violet distant mountains, dark evergreen pine forests, turquoise and deep teal lake, luminous broken golden reflection on water, reeds and rocks along the bottom corners, small cabin at upper right shore.
Composition: landscape 2:1 image. For gameplay, place all sky, sun, mountains, forest shoreline within the TOP 20 PERCENT of the image. The lower 80 percent is broad open clear teal lake water, darker toward bottom, unobstructed center for fish and hook. Tall pine trees may frame left and right margins. Warm reflection near center, subtle ripples. Shore vegetation restricted to corners. No boat, no people, no fish, no fishing line, no dock across central water. No UI, no buttons, no words, no watermark. Opaque background.

## Fishing equipment and boat

Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: transparent pixel-art game sprite sheet, six props.
Primary request: Create game props matching the detailed pixel graphics in the reference, especially the fishing equipment and wooden boat.
Composition: EXACTLY 3 columns by 2 rows, equal square cells with generous transparent gutters, one complete isolated object per cell. Top row: a diagonally angled fishing rod with cork handle and red-black reel; a standalone fishing reel with red spool and metallic silver frame; a curled red earthworm bait attached to small hook. Bottom row: a gold-orange fish-shaped fishing lure with hooks; a red and white fishing bobber with small brass tip; a SIDE-VIEW wooden rowboat facing right with seated fisherman in brown hat and red jacket, holding a rod pointing right, tiny glowing golden lantern on left end of boat.
Style: sharp detailed retro pixel art, crisp square pixels, dark outlines, rich shading, warm wood and golden metal colors, exact same style as reference. Boat should occupy broad width of its cell and fisherman and rod fit entirely inside. All six objects separate and fully contained.
Constraints: actual transparent alpha background, no labels, no cell borders, no interface frames, no shadows outside objects, no extra props, no water or scenery.
